# Consider It Sorted

Save what you love. Find it when it matters.

A smart organizer that makes sense of your Instagram saves and recommends where each piece of content belongs. It is a Chrome extension.

Built by Maya Agassi with Claude.

## Install

1. Download this project as a ZIP and open it.
2. Go to `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the folder you opened.
4. Go to instagram.com and click **Sort my saves**.

## Good to know

- It reads your saved posts slowly and sorts them in your browser. Your saves are not sent anywhere.
- It does not change anything on your Instagram account.
- Sorting uses keywords in English and Hebrew, so some posts may land in the wrong collection. You can move any post with its dropdown.
- This is an unofficial tool. Automated tools may go against Instagram's terms, so use it at your own risk. It stops if Instagram refuses a request.
